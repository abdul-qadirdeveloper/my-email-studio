# My Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business. We draft campaign plans, email sequences and individual emails here. Nothing is sent from this folder.

## Folders

- `brand/`: the business's voice, tone, colours, logo notes and anything else that keeps emails on-brand.
- `leads/`: notes about who we write to: audience segments and sign-up sources (no real contact lists).
- `campaigns/`: one subfolder per campaign, holding its plan and the drafted emails.
- `notes/`: research, decisions and reminders, such as which skills we chose and why.

## Rules

- Write in plain English. Use short sentences and everyday words, and avoid jargon.
- Never send email to real people. Draft and save emails only; do not call any sending service or API to deliver them.
- Ask me before deleting anything: files, folders or text I wrote.
