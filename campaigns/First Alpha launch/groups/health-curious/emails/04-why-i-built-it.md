I'm AQMA, the founder of YoYo40+. I built it for men who want to start moving but aren't sure how, and I want to tell you why.

I've seen how many people who work in offices sit for most of the day. After 40, a lot of them start to feel stiff and sore, and moving takes more effort than it used to. I wanted to help them keep moving, a little every day.

YoYo40+ is for men who are busy with work and family. Many of us aren't gym-goers, and we don't have an hour a day to spare.

So I wanted something smaller. 10 minutes a day. A daily walk around your street. A few strength and mobility moves.

YoYo40+ shows you which moves to do, reminds you each day and helps you plan your week. It asks a few questions about where you're starting from, then suggests a plan to fit. Simple tracking lets you see your steady progress.

My goal is simple. I want to keep walking on my own two feet well into my 80s. I built YoYo40+ to help you aim for the same.
