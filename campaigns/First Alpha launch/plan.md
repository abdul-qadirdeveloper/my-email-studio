# Campaign plan: YoYo40+ First Release launch

**Channel:** email only, to our waitlist. No paid ads.
**Dates:** 4 weeks, Monday 2 November to Sunday 29 November 2026.
**Brand rules:** every email follows `brand/voice.md`. Nothing is sent from this folder; these are plans and drafts only.

Items in `[square brackets]` are decisions we still need to make. See part 10.

---

## 1. Campaign overview

**Name:** Your Second Half, Strong

**In one sentence:** We invite our 2,000 waitlist members to start YoYo40+ with one small daily step, and to stay with it by starting a subscription.

**Main goal:** 300 waitlist members start a paid subscription by Sunday 29 November 2026. That is 15% of the waitlist.

**Other goals:**
- As many waitlist members as possible download the app and log a first 10-minute walk.
- We learn which of the three groups responds best, to shape the next campaign.
- We collect real user stories, with permission, for later emails.

## 2. Who we are writing to

About 2,000 working men aged 40 and over who joined the YoYo40+ waitlist. They are busy with jobs and family and want to stay strong, active and independent for the long run. Because they asked to hear from us, this is a warm audience.

We split them into three groups. Each one needs a slightly different message.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What may hold them back | Where they are |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Health-curious beginners** | Not exercising yet. Interested, but unsure where to start. | A simple, safe-feeling start that fits a busy day. | Feeling intimidated, unsure if it's "for them", worried about health issues. | Ready to try. Needs reassurance. |
| **B. Casual movers** | Exercise now and then, with no steady routine. | A routine they will actually keep. | Past starts and stops, little time. | Comparing. Needs a reason to commit. |
| **C. Daily exercisers** | Already move every day. | Strength, mobility and simple tracking to support what they already do. | "Do I need this? I already train." | Close to deciding. Needs to see what's extra for them. |

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** YoYo40+ is here. 10 minutes a day to support staying strong, active and on your own two feet.

**Supporting messages:**

1. **Built for busy men.** Routines take about 10 minutes a day, and no gym is needed. *Proof: the real length of the routines in the app.*
2. **Steady beats extreme.** Small daily steps you can keep, not a crash plan. *Proof: show the daily walking goal and the beginner routines.*
3. **Aim to keep walking strong at 80.** It's about staying independent and having energy for work and family. *Proof: none needed. This is a goal, never a promise.*
4. **Simple tracking.** See your steady progress at a glance. *Proof: screenshots of the real tracking screens.*

**By group:**
- **Group A:** "You don't need to be fit to start. Start with a 10-minute walk."
- **Group B:** "Keep it small enough to stick. One small step a day."
- **Group C:** "Add strength and mobility to what you already do, and track it in one place."

**Proof we may use:** only real facts about the app, such as routine length, features and price. Real beta user stories go in only with permission and with "Results vary. Your experience may differ." We use no health numbers, no research claims and no promised results (`brand/voice.md`, part 4).

## 7. How we'll measure success

**Main measure:** paid subscriptions from waitlist members by 29 November. **Target: 300.**

**Weekly checkpoints:** launch weeks usually bring the most sign-ups at the start, so we expect a front-loaded pattern. These are our own targets, not known benchmarks, because we have no past data yet.

| By end of | Subscriptions | Running total |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 (8 Nov) | 120 | 120 |
| Week 2 (15 Nov) | 80 | 200 |
| Week 3 (22 Nov) | 50 | 250 |
| Week 4 (29 Nov) | 50 | 300 |

**Other measures:**

| Measure | Why it matters | How we track it |
|---|---|---|
| Opens per email | Is the subject line working? | Email tool |
| Clicks per email | Is the email making people act? | Email tool |
| Downloads from the waitlist | The first step towards a subscription | Tracked store links |
| First walk logged | Shows real use, not just a download | App data |
| Download-to-subscription rate | Where people drop off | App data plus email tool |
| Unsubscribes per email | Are we sending too much? | Email tool |

Every measure is split by group (A, B, C).

**Reporting:** a short check every Monday before that day's email, and a full review on Monday 30 November.

## 9. Risks and what we'll do

| Risk | What we'll do |
|---|---|
| **The goal is high.** 300 is 15% of the waitlist in one month, and we have no past data to show that's realistic. | Watch the week 1 checkpoint. If we're well under 120 by 8 Nov, add a resend of E1 with a new subject line to those who didn't open it, and lean on whichever group is responding best. |
| **The waitlist has gone cold.** Some people joined long ago and may have forgotten us. | E1 reminds them why they signed up. Remove addresses that bounce. Check how old the list is before launch. |
| **App problems in a first release.** Bugs or crashes in week 1 would hurt trust. | Hold E1 if the app isn't stable. Keep a short "we've fixed it" email ready. Watch support messages closely in week 1. |
| **We don't know everyone's group.** | If tags are missing, add a one-click question to E1 ("Which sounds most like you?") and use the answers for E3. Untagged people get the Group A version. |
| **Breaking our own claim rules.** Health topics make it easy to over-promise. | Check every email with `/marketing:brand-review` and the quick test in `brand/voice.md` before it is approved. |

## 10. Next steps

**Decisions needed before writing emails:**
1. **Price and plans (decided):** the basic app is free. Premium is $10 a year, or $30 once for lifetime access.
2. **Launch offer (decided):** premium is free for the first month. After that it renews at $10 a year unless cancelled. There is no end date, so emails must not suggest one. Under our brand rules, we can't invent urgency.
3. `[Group tags]`: do we know each person's group?
4. `[Real user stories]`: do we have beta users willing to be quoted?
5. `[Where readers live]`: mostly US? (This affects the Thanksgiving week timing.)

**First tasks:**
1. Confirm the app goes live in both stores before 2 November.
2. Draft E1 and E2 with `/marketing:email-sequence`, then check them with `/marketing:brand-review`.
3. Set up tracked links and group tags in the email tool.
4. Write T1 and T2.

**Approvals:** the founder signs off on the final copy of each email before it's scheduled. Sending happens in the email tool, never from this folder.
