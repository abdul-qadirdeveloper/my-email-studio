- **Group A, health-curious beginners:** a simple start that feels safe. "You don't need to be fit to start."
- **Group B, casual movers:** a routine they'll actually keep, small enough to stick and fit a busy day.
- **Group C, daily exercisers:** what the app adds: strength, mobility and tracking in one place.
- **Angle A, a calm daily ritual:** 10 quiet minutes every day (`notes/angle-a.md`).
- **Angle B, discovering new teas:** pairs the 10-minute walk with trying a new tea (`notes/angle-b.md`).

- **To check:** "calm" and "quiet" may feel soft to Group C. Think about a stronger subject for their version.
