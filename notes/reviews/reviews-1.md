**#1 · ★★★★☆ (4/5)** · Mark T., 44, accountant
The daily walking goal is the first thing that's actually got me out at lunch. Hit 6,000 steps four days this week. Only gripe: the step count resets at midnight UTC, not my local time, so my evening walks sometimes land on the wrong day.

**#3 · ★★★★★ (5/5)** · Imran S., 41, software manager
The 10-minute mobility routine before my first call is now a habit. Clear video demos, calm voiceover, nobody shouting at me. Exactly what I wanted.
